    Stay safe

    • Never share an OTP, PIN or password — banks and apps never ask for them by SMS.
    • Don't open short links in messages about KYC, refunds, parcels or blocked accounts.
    • Prizes you never entered are fake. Messages that say “you won” and ask you to call or pay are scams.
    • Check urgency. “Today only”, “account will be blocked” and “urgent” are pressure tactics.
    • Verify through the official app or website instead of the number in the message.
